How to calculate weekly timesheet hours

For each day, the calculator adds the duration of Shift 1 and Shift 2, subtracts any unpaid break minutes, then combines the daily totals for the week.

Weekly formula

Weekly hours = sum of daily worked minutes ÷ 60

If your total exceeds the overtime threshold you select, the excess is shown separately as overtime.

Split shift example

If you work 9:00 AM–12:30 PM and 1:00 PM–5:30 PM, the two shifts total 8.00 hours. This is useful when your unpaid meal period is represented as a real clock-out and clock-in rather than a break deduction.

Overnight shifts

If an end time is earlier than its start time, that shift is treated as continuing past midnight into the next day.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use both split shifts and unpaid breaks?

Yes. The calculator first adds both shifts and then subtracts the unpaid break minutes you enter.

Does it calculate overtime?

Yes. Choose the weekly overtime threshold and multiplier that apply to your use case.

Can I export the weekly time card?

Yes. You can print it or download the daily rows as CSV.
