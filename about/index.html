About

Simple work-time tools without an account.

Hosted Timesheets is a collection of free browser-based tools for calculating timesheets, work hours, lunch breaks, overtime and related timekeeping tasks.

Why we built it

Many people only need to add a timesheet or convert work time. They should not have to create an account or upload employee information just to get a calculation.

Privacy-first calculations

Our calculators are designed to process the values you enter in your browser. We do not require a user profile to use the core calculators.

What you can calculate

Hosted Timesheets includes tools for weekly and biweekly timesheets, work hours, lunch-break deductions, decimal payroll hours, overtime estimates, PTO accrual and billable hours. Each tool is built around a focused task so you can get a result quickly without setting up an account or importing employee records.

Where a calculation depends on assumptions, the page explains the inputs and limitations so you can compare the result with your employer, payroll provider or other authoritative records.

Accuracy and scope

We test formulas and explain assumptions, but results are estimates. Employment, payroll, tax and overtime rules can vary by employer and jurisdiction. Always verify payroll or legal decisions with an appropriate authoritative source.

How we build the tools

Calculation methodology and editorial standards

Hosted Timesheets is designed around transparent arithmetic: the input fields, formulas, assumptions and limitations should be understandable without creating an account.

Focused calculators, not black-box answers

Each calculator is scoped to a specific timekeeping task such as adding weekly punches, subtracting unpaid breaks, converting minutes to decimal hours, estimating overtime pay or projecting PTO. Where a result depends on a user-selected assumption, the page identifies that assumption instead of presenting it as a universal rule.

Worked examples are used to check the math

We review calculator behavior against hand-worked examples for ordinary shifts, lunch deductions, overnight work, decimal conversion and other supported cases. Related guides show the same arithmetic in prose so users can reproduce important results independently.

Payroll and leave rules are kept separate from arithmetic

Timekeeping, overtime, PTO and payroll requirements can vary by employer, contract and jurisdiction. Hosted Timesheets does not choose a legal overtime threshold, decide whether a meal period is paid, determine PTO eligibility or replace an employer's payroll or HR system. The tools calculate from the inputs and settings supplied by the user.

Privacy by design

Core calculations run in the browser and do not require an employee account. Users should still avoid entering information they do not need for a calculation. Our Privacy Policy explains the site-level services and data practices that may apply when visiting the website.

Corrections and feedback

If a calculation explanation is unclear or a supported scenario produces an unexpected result, use the Contact page and include the inputs needed to reproduce the issue without sending sensitive employee information. We use reproducible examples to review corrections and improve the relevant page.
