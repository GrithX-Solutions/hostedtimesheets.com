Methodology: how to track time off

Start with the opening balance for each leave category, add any time earned during the period and subtract time already used.

Current balance = opening balance + earned − used

If future leave is already scheduled, subtract it separately to see what remains available after those plans.

Available after scheduled = current balance − scheduled leave

Example in hours

If your PTO opening balance is 40 hours, you earn 24 hours, use 16 hours and schedule another 8 hours, your current balance is 48 hours and your available balance after scheduled leave is 40 hours.

Example with a scheduled shortfall

If a leave category starts at 8 hours, earns 0, has 4 hours already used and 10 hours scheduled, the current balance is 4 hours and the available-after-scheduled balance is −6 hours. The tracker reports a 6-hour shortfall instead of changing that result to zero.

Track hours or days consistently

You can use the same arithmetic in hours or days. The unit selector changes labels only; it does not convert values. If you switch from 8 hours to days, the number stays 8, so choose the correct unit before interpreting the balance.

Why separate leave categories?

Some employers maintain separate balances for vacation, sick leave, personal leave or other paid time off. Keeping categories separate makes it easier to compare your records with a policy or payroll system.

Private-by-default browser workflow

The tracker does not persist balances by default. If you choose Save in this browser, it stores the four category rows and selected unit in local browser storage on this device. Clear saved data removes that local copy. The tracker does not create an account or approval workflow.

Print, copy and CSV export

You can print the tracker, copy a plain-text summary, or download the current rows as CSV for a spreadsheet. Export only after checking the figures against the authoritative leave or payroll system.

Frequently asked questions

Does this tracker save my balances?

Not by default. Your entries remain on the page only unless you choose Save in this browser. That option stores the tracker in local browser storage on the current device until you clear it.

Can I track PTO in hours or days?

Yes. Choose Hours or Days before entering balances. Changing the unit label does not convert existing numbers, so use one consistent unit for all rows.

What does a negative time-off balance mean here?

It means the entered used or scheduled time exceeds the numeric balance for that category. The tracker shows the shortfall instead of hiding it, but it does not decide whether a negative balance is allowed by a policy.

Is scheduled leave already deducted from current balance?

No. Current balance reflects opening balance plus earned time minus used time. Scheduled leave is shown separately in the available-after-scheduled balance.

Does this tracker calculate PTO accrual automatically?

No. Enter earned time from the policy or authoritative system. Use the PTO Accrual Calculator when you need arithmetic for an hourly, pay-period or fixed accrual method.

Can I export my time-off tracker?

Yes. You can copy a text summary, print the tracker, or download the current rows as CSV.

Does the tracker send my entered balances to Hosted Timesheets?

The tracker logic processes the entered balances in your browser and does not submit those values to Hosted Timesheets. If you choose local saving, the values are stored in this browser on this device.

Can I use this for official company payroll or leave records?

Use it for personal planning and reconciliation. Verify official balances, approvals and policy rules against the employer, payroll or leave-management system.

Leave balance tracking

Keep available, used and scheduled time off distinct

A useful leave tracker answers more than “how much PTO is left?” It should show what is available now, what has already been used, and what is planned but not yet taken.

Use separate categories when the policy separates balances

Vacation, sick time, personal leave and other categories may have different earning or usage rules. Tracking them separately avoids masking a shortage in one category with unused time from another. Combine categories only when the employer's policy actually treats them as one bank.

Opening balance and earned time should be traceable

Enter a starting balance that matches the last verified HR or payroll statement, then add the time earned or otherwise credited since that point. If the tracker and payroll disagree, reconcile from the last known balance instead of overwriting the current number without identifying the difference.

Scheduled time is not always the same as used time

A future approved vacation can reduce the amount you consider available for planning even though it has not yet been deducted from the official used balance. Keeping scheduled leave visible helps prevent double-booking or assuming the entire current balance is uncommitted.

Example balance check

If a vacation bank starts at 32 hours, earns 4 more, has 8 hours already used and 16 hours scheduled for next month, the current mathematical balance is 28 hours while the uncommitted planning balance would be 12 hours. Whether scheduled leave should reduce an official displayed balance depends on the system or policy.

Do not hide category deficits

A zero floor can make a tracker look safer than the arithmetic actually is. If used or scheduled time exceeds one category’s numeric balance, this tracker keeps the negative result visible and separately totals the scheduled shortfall. Another category’s positive balance does not erase that shortage.

Browser-only saving is optional

Many leave-management systems require accounts because they coordinate people, policies and approvals. This tool has a narrower job: personal balance reconciliation. By default it does not persist the entered balances. Local saving is opt-in and remains on the current browser/device until cleared.

Export after reconciliation

Use print, copy or CSV output as a planning record after checking the values against the authoritative leave system. This browser tool is not an approval workflow and does not determine eligibility, accrual rules, carryover, forfeiture or statutory leave requirements.
