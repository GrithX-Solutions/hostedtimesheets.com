What this timesheet template includes

The template provides one row per day with fields for clock-in, clock-out, unpaid break minutes and total hours. Switch between weekly and biweekly layouts without creating an account.

Printable and spreadsheet-friendly

Use the print button for paper or PDF output, or download CSV to continue working in Excel, Google Sheets or another spreadsheet application.

Who can use it?

Employees, freelancers, contractors and small businesses can use the template for simple time records. For payroll or compliance use, verify that the format meets your employer and jurisdiction requirements.
