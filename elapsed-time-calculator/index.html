Quick answer

Elapsed time is the amount of time between a starting point and an ending point.

Elapsed time = end time − start time

Example: from 9:00 AM to 5:30 PM is 8 hours 30 minutes, or 8.50 decimal hours.

How to calculate elapsed time

For two times on the same day, subtract the start time from the end time. When an end time is earlier than the start time and no dates are entered, this calculator treats the end as occurring on the next day. That makes overnight durations such as 10:00 PM to 6:00 AM work without extra steps.

Manual elapsed-time calculation

When the ending minutes are smaller than the starting minutes, borrow one hour as 60 minutes before subtracting. For example, from 8:45 AM to 12:10 PM, rewrite 12:10 as 11 hours 70 minutes. Then 70 − 45 = 25 minutes and 11 − 8 = 3 hours, so the elapsed time is 3 hours 25 minutes.

You can use the same method with 24-hour time. For example, 13:20 to 17:05 equals 3 hours 45 minutes.

Elapsed time across dates

Enter both a start date and an end date when the duration spans more than one day. The calculator combines the date difference with the start and end times, then reports the total duration in days, hours and minutes as well as decimal hours, total minutes and total seconds.

Subtracting a break

The optional break field subtracts minutes from the elapsed duration. For example, an 8-hour-30-minute span with a 30-minute unpaid break produces 8 hours of net time. The gross elapsed duration remains visible separately.

Elapsed time vs. time duration

Elapsed time and time duration both describe the span between a starting point and an ending point. This time duration calculator reports that span in days, hours, minutes, decimal hours and total minutes so the same result can be used for work-hour checks or general duration calculations.

Methodology and assumptions

Date inputs are treated as plain calendar dates and times, not as geographic time zones. The calculator does not convert between cities, daylight-saving rules or UTC offsets. When both dates are blank, an end time earlier than the start time is treated as the following day. Break time cannot reduce net time below zero.

Elapsed time for work hours

For a single shift, elapsed time can help check the span between clock-in and clock-out. For a full weekly time card with daily breaks and overtime, use the Time Card Calculator or Work Hours Calculator.

Frequently asked questions

How many hours are between 9 AM and 5 PM?

There are 8 elapsed hours between 9:00 AM and 5:00 PM on the same day.

How do I calculate elapsed time overnight?

If the end time is after midnight, count from the start time to midnight and then from midnight to the end time. This calculator does that automatically when both dates are blank and the end time is earlier than the start time.

Can I subtract a lunch or break?

Yes. Enter the break length in minutes. The calculator shows both the full elapsed duration and the net duration after the break.

Can this calculator handle multiple days?

Yes. Enter both a start date and an end date to calculate a duration that spans multiple calendar days.

Does this calculate time-zone differences?

No. It calculates duration from the dates and times you enter without converting between geographic time zones.

How do I calculate elapsed time when the end minutes are smaller?

Borrow one hour as 60 minutes before subtracting. For example, 8:45 AM to 12:10 PM becomes 8:45 to 11:70, which equals 3 hours 25 minutes.
