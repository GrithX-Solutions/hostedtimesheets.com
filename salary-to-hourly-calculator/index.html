Quick answer

Convert annual salary to hourly pay by dividing annual gross salary by the paid hours represented by that salary.

Hourly equivalent = annual gross salary ÷ (hours per paid workweek × paid weeks per year)

Example: $52,000 ÷ (40 × 52) = $25.00 per hour.

How to convert annual salary to an hourly rate

1. Calculate annual paid hours

Multiply hours per paid workweek by the number of paid weeks represented by the annual salary. A 40-hour schedule over 52 paid weeks equals 2,080 annual hours. A 37.5-hour schedule over 52 paid weeks equals 1,950 annual hours.

Annual paid hours = hours per paid workweek × paid weeks per year

2. Divide annual gross salary by annual paid hours

Once annual paid hours are known, divide the annual gross salary by that total. The result is an hourly gross-pay equivalent, not a legal classification or a promise of an hourly wage.

Hourly equivalent = annual gross salary ÷ annual paid hours

Paid weeks are not the same as calendar weeks worked

Use 52 paid weeks when the annual salary covers the full year, including paid vacation and paid holidays. Do not subtract paid leave simply because you are away from work. Reduce the paid-week input only when the annual gross amount itself represents fewer paid weeks.

If unpaid leave reduces the amount you will actually receive, use the gross annual amount that matches those paid weeks. The calculator does not decide whether leave should be paid.

Why monthly and biweekly equivalents use different divisors

The average monthly equivalent is always annual salary divided by 12. It is not weekly pay multiplied by four, because a calendar month is longer than four weeks on average.

The biweekly equivalent on this page means two of the paid workweeks you entered. With 52 paid weeks it equals annual salary divided by 26. If you need a paycheck-frequency calculation with pay-period assumptions, use the Biweekly Pay Calculator.

37.5-hour and nonstandard workweeks

A 2,080-hour year is only a shortcut for 40 hours across 52 paid weeks. A 37.5-hour week across 52 paid weeks is 1,950 hours. For example, $60,000 ÷ 1,950 = about $30.77 per hour.

The same method works for 30, 32, 36, 45 or another weekly schedule. This page accepts decimal weekly hours so the divisor can match the paid schedule more closely.

Convert monthly salary to hourly pay

If you know monthly gross salary first, multiply it by 12 to get an annual amount. Then divide by annual paid hours. For example, $5,000 per month becomes $60,000 per year, which is about $28.85 per hour at 40 hours per week for 52 paid weeks.

Frequently asked questions

How do I convert annual salary to hourly pay?

Divide annual gross salary by annual paid hours. Annual paid hours equal hours per paid workweek multiplied by paid weeks per year.

What is $60,000 a year hourly?

At 40 hours per week for 52 paid weeks, $60,000 divided by 2,080 hours is about $28.85 per hour.

What is $50,000 a year hourly?

At 40 hours per week for 52 paid weeks, $50,000 divided by 2,080 hours is about $24.04 per hour.

How do I convert salary to hourly for 37.5 hours a week?

At 37.5 hours per week for 52 paid weeks, annual paid hours are 1,950. A $60,000 annual salary is therefore about $30.77 per hour.

Should I always divide salary by 2,080?

No. The 2,080-hour shortcut applies only to 40 hours per week across 52 paid weeks. Use the schedule represented by the annual salary you enter.

What should I enter for paid weeks per year?

Use 52 when the annual salary covers a full paid year, including paid vacation or holidays. Use fewer weeks only when the annual gross amount represents fewer paid weeks.

How do I convert a monthly salary to hourly pay?

Multiply monthly gross salary by 12 to get an annual amount, then divide by annual paid hours. For example, $5,000 per month is $60,000 per year before the hourly conversion.

Does this calculate take-home pay or exchange rates?

No. Results are gross-pay equivalents before taxes, deductions and benefits. Currency selection changes formatting only and does not convert exchange rates.
