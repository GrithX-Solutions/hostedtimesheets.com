Quick answer

To convert an hourly wage to annual gross pay, multiply the hourly rate by regular hours per week, then multiply by the number of paid workweeks per year.

Annual gross pay = hourly wage × hours per week × weeks per year

Example: $25 × 40 × 52 = $52,000 per year before overtime, taxes, deductions and benefits.

Hourly wage to annual salary formula

This hourly pay-to-salary calculator uses your actual schedule instead of assuming every worker has the same year. A 40-hour week across 52 paid weeks equals 2,080 regular hours, so you can use the shortcut hourly wage × 2,080 only when that schedule fits.

At 40 hours per week for 52 weeks, $20 per hour equals $41,600 per year, $25 per hour equals $52,000, $30 per hour equals $62,400, and $35 per hour equals $72,800 in regular gross pay.

Hourly pay to weekly, biweekly and monthly income

Weekly regular pay equals hourly wage multiplied by regular weekly hours. A simple biweekly equivalent is twice the weekly amount. For an average monthly equivalent, calculate annual gross pay first and divide by 12.

Weekly pay = hourly wage × weekly hours

Biweekly pay = weekly pay × 2

Average monthly pay = annual gross pay ÷ 12

These are gross-pay equivalents. An employer's actual paycheck can differ because payroll calendars, unpaid time, overtime, deductions and benefits vary.

Use your real hours and paid workweeks

The common 2,080-hour shortcut assumes 40 paid hours every week for all 52 weeks. If you work 32 hours per week, take unpaid weeks off, or have seasonal work, enter those actual values instead.

For example, $25 per hour at 40 hours per week for 50 paid weeks equals $50,000 per year. The same hourly rate across 52 paid weeks equals $52,000.

Optional overtime estimate

You can add expected overtime hours and the multiplier you want to use. Enter only regular hours in the regular-hours field; overtime hours are additional. If you include the same overtime hours in both fields, the calculator will count them once as regular pay and again as overtime pay.

Weekly overtime pay = hourly wage × overtime multiplier × overtime hours per week

Annual pay including overtime = annual regular pay + (weekly overtime pay × weeks per year)

Overtime eligibility and the rate that applies depend on the relevant rules and work arrangement. For a more focused breakdown of regular hours, overtime hours and overtime pay, use the Overtime Calculator.

Methodology and assumptions

Regular pay uses the hourly rate, regular weekly hours and paid workweeks you enter. Daily regular pay divides weekly regular pay by the days-per-week value. Biweekly regular pay is twice the weekly regular amount. Monthly regular pay is the annual regular-pay estimate divided by 12. Optional overtime uses the overtime hours and multiplier entered separately, and only the weekly and annual “including OT” metrics add that overtime amount.

The calculator enforces 1–53 weeks per year and 1–7 workdays per week in its arithmetic, matching the visible input limits. Other numeric inputs are clamped at zero, while the overtime multiplier cannot fall below 1.

The calculator reports gross-pay arithmetic only. It does not estimate taxes, deductions, benefits, paid leave value or overtime eligibility. That matters because current search results for hourly wage and hourly pay calculators often mix gross-conversion tools with paycheck and take-home-tax calculators; this page intentionally answers the gross hourly-to-pay conversion question. Use the Salary to Hourly Calculator when you need the reverse conversion from annual salary to an hourly equivalent.

Frequently asked questions

How much is $20 an hour annually?

At 40 hours per week for 52 weeks, $20 per hour equals $41,600 in regular gross annual pay.

How much is $25 an hour annually?

At 40 hours per week for 52 weeks, $25 per hour equals $52,000 in regular gross annual pay.

How do I convert an hourly wage to monthly pay?

Calculate annual gross pay first, then divide by 12 for an average monthly equivalent.

Why does hourly-to-annual pay often use 2,080 hours?

Two thousand eighty hours is 40 hours per week multiplied by 52 weeks. It is a convenient full-year assumption, not a universal schedule, so use your actual weekly hours and paid workweeks when they differ.

Does this calculator include taxes?

No. It calculates gross wage equivalents before taxes, deductions and benefits.

Should I include overtime in regular hours per week?

No. Enter regular hours in the regular-hours field and add expected overtime separately. Otherwise the same overtime hours would be counted once as regular pay and again as overtime pay.
