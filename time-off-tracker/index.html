Methodology: how to track time off

Start with the opening balance for each leave category, add any time earned during the period and subtract time already used.

Current balance = opening balance + earned − used

If future leave is already scheduled, subtract it separately to see what remains available after those plans.

Available after scheduled = current balance − scheduled leave

Example

If your PTO opening balance is 40 hours, you earn 24 hours, use 16 hours and schedule another 8 hours, your current balance is 48 hours and your available balance after scheduled leave is 40 hours.

Why separate leave categories?

Some employers maintain separate balances for vacation, sick leave, personal leave or other paid time off. Keeping categories separate makes it easier to compare your records with a policy or payroll system.

Frequently asked questions

Does this tracker save my balances?

No. This version calculates in your browser and does not provide persistent account storage.

Is scheduled leave already deducted from current balance?

No. Current balance reflects earned minus used time. Scheduled leave is shown separately so you can see availability after future plans.

Can I use this for company payroll records?

It can help with personal reconciliation, but official balances should be verified against your employer or payroll system.

Leave balance tracking

Keep available, used and scheduled time off distinct

A useful leave tracker answers more than “how much PTO is left?” It should show what is available now, what has already been used, and what is planned but not yet taken.

Use separate categories when the policy separates balances

Vacation, sick time, personal leave and other categories may have different earning or usage rules. Tracking them separately avoids masking a shortage in one category with unused time from another. Combine categories only when the employer's policy actually treats them as one bank.

Opening balance and earned time should be traceable

Enter a starting balance that matches the last verified HR or payroll statement, then add the time earned or otherwise credited since that point. If the tracker and payroll disagree, reconcile from the last known balance instead of overwriting the current number without identifying the difference.

Scheduled time is not always the same as used time

A future approved vacation can reduce the amount you consider available for planning even though it has not yet been deducted from the official used balance. Keeping scheduled leave visible helps prevent double-booking or assuming the entire current balance is uncommitted.

Example balance check

If a vacation bank starts at 32 hours, earns 4 more, has 8 hours already used and 16 hours scheduled for next month, the current mathematical balance is 28 hours while the uncommitted planning balance would be 12 hours. Whether scheduled leave should reduce an official displayed balance depends on the system or policy.

Export after reconciliation

Use print or CSV output as a planning record after checking the values against the authoritative leave system. This browser tool is not an approval workflow and does not determine eligibility, carryover, forfeiture or statutory leave requirements.
