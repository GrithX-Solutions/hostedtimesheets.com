Methodology and assumptions

The hourly equivalent uses the annual salary, weekly hours and workweeks entered. The familiar 2,080-hour shortcut applies only to 40 hours per week for 52 weeks. Results are gross-pay arithmetic and do not determine employment classification, taxes or deductions.

How to convert annual salary to an hourly rate

Divide annual gross salary by the number of hours you expect to work during the year. Annual work hours are weekly hours multiplied by weeks worked.

Hourly rate = annual salary ÷ (hours per week × weeks per year)

Example: $52,000 salary

At 40 hours per week for 52 weeks, annual work time is 2,080 hours. A $52,000 salary divided by 2,080 hours equals $25 per hour.

Convert salary to hourly for a 37.5-hour workweek

Do not use the 2,080-hour shortcut when the schedule is 37.5 hours per week. A 37.5-hour week across 52 weeks equals 1,950 annual hours. For example, $60,000 ÷ 1,950 = about $30.77 per hour.

Hourly equivalent = annual salary ÷ 1,950 for a 37.5-hour, 52-week schedule.

Why weeks worked matters

The common 2,080-hour shortcut assumes 40 hours every week for all 52 weeks. If your schedule uses fewer paid workweeks or a different weekly schedule, the hourly equivalent changes. This calculator lets you use the schedule that matches your situation.

Part-time and compressed schedules

A salaried role can still be converted using 30, 32, 36 or another weekly-hour figure. The calculation is arithmetic only; it does not determine whether a worker is legally salaried, exempt or nonexempt.

Salary to daily and biweekly pay

The daily equivalent divides weekly gross pay by workdays per week. The biweekly equivalent is two weeks of gross pay. These figures are useful for offer comparisons and budgeting, but they are not a net paycheck estimate.

Frequently asked questions

What is $60,000 a year hourly?

At 40 hours per week for 52 weeks, $60,000 divided by 2,080 hours is about $28.85 per hour.

What is $50,000 a year hourly?

Using a 40-hour, 52-week schedule, $50,000 is about $24.04 per hour.

Should I always use 2,080 hours?

No. Use 2,080 only when 40 hours per week and 52 workweeks accurately represent the schedule.

Does this include taxes?

No. Results are gross-pay equivalents before taxes, deductions and benefits.

How do I convert salary to hourly for a 37.5-hour week?

Multiply 37.5 hours by the number of workweeks, then divide annual salary by that total. At 52 weeks, 37.5 × 52 = 1,950 hours, so $60,000 per year is about $30.77 per hour.
