Quick answer

To convert time to decimal hours, convert the full duration to seconds and divide by 3,600. With whole hours and minutes only, the same formula is hours + (minutes ÷ 60). This calculator also converts decimal hours back to hours, minutes and seconds.

How the hours-to-decimal calculation works

One hour contains 3,600 seconds. The calculator first keeps the source duration exact, then formats the decimal result only after the math is complete.

Total seconds = hours × 3,600 + minutes × 60 + seconds

Decimal hours = total seconds ÷ 3,600

Example: 8 hours 1 minute

8h 1m is 28,860 seconds. Dividing 28,860 by 3,600 gives 8.016666… hours. At four decimal places the displayed result is 8.0167. At two places it is 8.02, but the underlying duration is still 481 exact minutes.

Example with seconds

7h 45m 30s is 27,930 seconds. Divide by 3,600 and the exact value is 7.758333… hours, displayed as 7.7583 at four decimal places. Including seconds avoids discarding sub-minute time before conversion.

Convert decimal hours back to time

For reverse conversion, multiply the decimal-hour input by 3,600. The calculator rounds that result once to the nearest whole second, then splits it into hours, minutes and seconds.

Rounded seconds = round(decimal hours × 3,600)

Example: 7.75 decimal hours

7.75 × 3,600 = 27,900 seconds. That normalizes to 7h 45m 0s. Totals above 24 hours remain accumulated durations, so 30.25 decimal hours becomes 30h 15m 0s rather than wrapping to a time of day.

Precision and estimated pay

The 2, 3 or 4-place selector controls only the displayed and copied decimal result. It does not round the source duration. If you enter an hourly rate, the pay estimate uses the exact source time in Time → Decimal mode or the exact decimal input in Decimal → Time mode, then rounds currency only at the final money display.

For example, 8h 1m at $25 per hour uses 481 ÷ 60 × $25 and displays $200.42. Multiplying the two-place display 8.02 by $25 would produce a different number, so the calculator keeps exact source time authoritative.

Clock notation is not decimal notation

8:30 means eight hours and thirty minutes, which equals 8.50 decimal hours. Writing 8.30 decimal hours means eight hours plus 0.30 of an hour, or 8h 18m. The decimal digits represent a fraction of an hour, not clock minutes.

Payroll rounding is a separate rule

This tool performs mathematical conversion only. It does not decide whether a payroll, billing or timekeeping system should round punches or durations. If your system specifies a rounding method, apply that documented rule to the source records separately.

FAQ

How do I convert hours and minutes to decimal hours?

Multiply whole hours by 3,600, add minutes × 60 and any seconds, then divide total seconds by 3,600. With no seconds, this is the familiar hours + minutes ÷ 60 formula.

Is 30 minutes 0.30 hours?

No. Thirty minutes is half of a 60-minute hour, so 30 ÷ 60 = 0.50. A decimal value of 0.30 hour equals 18 minutes.

Can I include seconds?

Yes. Seconds are divided by 3,600. For example, 7 hours 45 minutes 30 seconds equals 7.7583 hours when shown to four decimal places.

How do I convert decimal hours back to time?

Multiply decimal hours by 3,600, round to the nearest whole second, then split the total into hours, minutes and seconds. For example, 7.75 hours becomes 7h 45m 0s.

Why can pay use more precision than the displayed decimal?

The selected 2, 3 or 4 decimal places change display and copy formatting only. Pay uses the exact source duration or exact decimal input before the final currency rounding.

Does this calculator apply payroll rounding?

No. It performs mathematical time conversion only. Your payroll or timekeeping system may apply separate rounding rules, so follow the documented policy for the records you submit.

Methodology

Exact source time stays authoritative

The calculator separates conversion math from display formatting so repeated decimals do not silently change later calculations.

Forward conversion

Whole hours, minutes and seconds become one exact total in seconds before division. Minutes must be whole numbers from 0 to 59, seconds must be whole numbers from 0 to 59, and whole hours must be a nonnegative integer. Invalid values are shown as errors rather than silently clamped.

Reverse conversion

Decimal hours can include values above 24. The input is multiplied by 3,600 and normalized to the nearest whole second for the time display. The optional pay estimate still uses the original decimal input rather than the nearest-second display value.

Display precision

Two, three and four decimal places are available for the primary forward result. Changing the selector never changes total seconds, source duration or pay. Keep enough precision for the destination system rather than assuming two decimal places are always a payroll rule.
