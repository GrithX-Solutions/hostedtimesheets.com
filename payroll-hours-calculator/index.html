How payroll hours are converted

Payroll systems often use decimal hours rather than hours-and-minutes notation. Each day's minutes are divided by 60 and added to the whole hours.

Decimal payroll hours = hours + (minutes ÷ 60)

Example

8 hours 15 minutes equals 8.25 payroll hours. 7 hours 45 minutes equals 7.75 payroll hours.

Weekly payroll total

Pay estimate

Regular hours are multiplied by the regular hourly rate. Overtime hours use the regular rate multiplied by your chosen overtime multiplier.

Frequently asked questions

Is 8 hours 30 minutes entered as 8.30?

No. Enter 8 in the hours field and 30 in the minutes field. The calculator converts that to 8.50 decimal hours.

Does this replace a payroll system?

No. It is a time-conversion and pay-estimation utility. Official payroll can include deductions, taxes, benefits, rounding rules and jurisdiction-specific requirements.

Can I change the overtime threshold?

Yes. Enter the threshold and multiplier that apply to your use case.
