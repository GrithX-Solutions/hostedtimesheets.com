Choose the right calculator

Different timekeeping questions need different inputs

Hosted Timesheets separates common tasks so you do not have to force every problem into one large form. Start with the source information you actually have: clock punches, a duration, payroll minutes, PTO policy values or billable time.

Clock-in and clock-out records

Use the Time Card & Timesheet Calculator for one clock-in/out pair per day across a week. Use the Weekly Timesheet Calculator for split shifts, the Biweekly Timesheet Calculator for a 14-day period, or the Lunch Break calculator for lunch-specific examples.

Repeated shifts or a planned clock-out

Use the Work Hours Calculator when the same start, end and break repeat across several days. Use the Clock Out Time Calculator when you know the start time and paid-work target and need the planned leaving time.

Payroll and decimal conversion

Use the Decimal Hours Calculator or Payroll Hours Calculator when the worked duration is already known and the goal is to convert minutes into a payroll-friendly decimal format.

How the calculators produce results

The core calculators run in the browser and calculate from the values you enter. They use transparent arithmetic such as subtracting clock-in from clock-out, subtracting unpaid break minutes, converting minutes by dividing by 60, or multiplying eligible hours by a PTO accrual rate. Related guides show worked examples so important results can be checked manually.

What the site does not assume for you

Timekeeping and payroll rules are not universal. The tools do not decide whether a meal period is paid, which overtime threshold is legally required, whether overtime hours accrue PTO, how taxes should be withheld, or what leave policy applies to a particular employee. Where a threshold, multiplier, rate, cap or rounding option is configurable, use the value that matches the authoritative policy or system you are working with.

When to use the result as an estimate

Pay, overtime, PTO and billable-revenue outputs are planning or reconciliation estimates unless they have been verified against the governing record. Before payroll, invoicing or an employee balance is finalized, compare the calculator result with approved punches, the payroll or HR system, the applicable policy and any required legal or contractual rules.

Privacy-first by default

You do not need an account to use the core tools, and the calculations are designed to happen on your device. Enter only the information needed for the calculation and avoid adding unnecessary sensitive employee details. See the Privacy Policy for site-level data practices and the About page for our calculation methodology and scope.
