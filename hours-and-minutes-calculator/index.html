Hours & Minutes Calculator

Add, subtract or total hour-and-minute durations, or convert one duration into total minutes and decimal hours. Optionally enter an hourly rate for a simple rate × duration estimate.

Add, subtract or total hours and minutes

Use known durations such as 2h 45m. For clock-in and clock-out times, use the Time Duration or Work Hours calculators instead.

Multi-duration mode supports up to 10 durations total. Each duration after the first can be added or subtracted.

Hourly rate is optional. Currency changes number formatting only; it does not convert the entered rate or apply exchange rates.

Quick answer

Use this hours and minutes calculator to add two durations, subtract one duration from another, or convert a single duration to total minutes and decimal hours.

Total minutes = (hours × 60) + minutes

For addition, add the two total-minute values. For subtraction, subtract the second total-minute value from the first, then convert the signed result back to hours and minutes.

Add hours and minutes

Convert each duration to minutes, add the totals, then normalize the result back into hours and minutes.

Result minutes = first duration minutes + second duration minutes

Example: 2h 45m is 165 minutes and 1h 20m is 80 minutes. Together they equal 245 minutes, which is 4h 05m or about 4.0833 decimal hours.

Total multiple durations

Select Add/subtract multiple durations when you need more than two entries. The first duration is the starting value. Every later row can add or subtract a duration, and the calculator supports up to 10 durations total.

Example: 7h 45m + 8h 20m + 6h 10m − 0h 30m = 21h 45m = 1,305 minutes = 21.75 decimal hours.

This mode is useful for combining task, project, study or already-calculated work durations. It still treats every input as a duration, not a clock time.

Subtract hours and minutes

Subtraction uses the same total-minute method.

Result minutes = first duration minutes − second duration minutes

Example: 3h 10m − 1h 45m = 1h 25m. If the second duration is longer, the result stays negative; 1h 15m − 2h 00m = −0h 45m.

Convert hours and minutes

To convert one duration to total minutes, multiply whole hours by 60 and add the minutes. Divide total minutes by 60 for decimal hours.

Decimal hours = total minutes ÷ 60

Example: 8h 30m = 510 total minutes = 8.50 decimal hours.

Decimal precision and optional rate × time estimate

The decimal-hours result shows at least two and up to four decimal places, so values such as 6h 20m display as 6.3333 instead of being limited to 6.33. If you enter an hourly rate, the estimate uses the exact total-minutes ÷ 60 value before display rounding.

Rate × time estimate = (result minutes ÷ 60) × hourly rate

If the hourly-rate field is blank, the duration calculation remains valid and the estimate shows an em dash. A negative duration produces a signed arithmetic estimate. This calculator does not decide payable time, overtime, taxes, billing rules or payroll rounding.

Selecting another currency changes the displayed money format only. It does not convert the entered hourly rate or apply a foreign-exchange rate.

Duration math is different from clock-time math

This page operates on durations you already know. It does not calculate the difference between 9:00 AM and 5:30 PM. Use the Time Duration Calculator for a general start/end time difference or the Work Hours Calculator for work shifts and breaks.

Methodology and input behavior

Each hours field must contain a whole number at or above zero. Each minutes field is the minute portion of a duration and must be a whole number from 0 through 59. Invalid required inputs show a field error and suppress stale results instead of being silently clamped or converted to another value.

Addition carries every 60 minutes into an hour automatically. Subtraction preserves the sign when the result is below zero. Multi-duration mode applies the selected plus or minus operation to each later row in sequence.

The optional hourly rate may be left blank. If entered, it must be a finite nonnegative number. The calculator performs duration arithmetic only and does not determine overtime, payroll rounding, billing rules, taxes or whether a duration is compensable time.

Frequently asked questions

Is 30 minutes equal to 0.30 hours?

No. Thirty minutes is half an hour, so it equals 0.50 decimal hours.

How do I add hours and minutes?

Convert each duration to total minutes, add the totals, then convert the result back to hours and minutes. For example, 2h 45m + 1h 20m = 4h 05m.

Can the subtraction result be negative?

Yes. If the second duration is longer than the first, the calculator keeps the result negative instead of flooring it at zero.

How many minutes are in 7.5 hours?

7.5 × 60 = 450 minutes.

Can this calculate pay too?

Yes. Enter an hourly rate and the calculator multiplies the exact minute-derived decimal hours by that rate.

Does estimated pay use the rounded decimal-hours display?

No. Estimated pay uses total minutes divided by 60 before the decimal-hours result is rounded for display.

Can I total more than two durations?

Yes. Select Add/subtract multiple durations to combine up to 10 durations. Each duration after the first can be added or subtracted.

Does changing currency convert the hourly rate?

No. Currency selection changes money formatting only. It does not apply an exchange rate or convert the hourly rate you entered.

Duration arithmetic

Keep hour-and-minute math in base 60

Minutes roll over at 60, not 100. Converting durations to total minutes first makes addition, subtraction and decimal conversion consistent.

Why 7h 45m + 8h 20m is not 15.65 hours

Forty-five minutes plus 20 minutes equals 65 minutes, which carries to 1 hour 5 minutes. The duration total is therefore 16h 05m. If you need decimal hours, convert the 5-minute remainder with 5 ÷ 60 rather than writing the clock minutes after a decimal point.

Signed subtraction keeps the arithmetic visible

If you subtract a larger duration from a smaller one, this calculator returns a negative duration instead of silently replacing it with zero. For example, 45 minutes − 1h 15m = −30 minutes, shown as −0h 30m and −0.50 decimal hours.

Pay uses the unrounded duration

For pay estimation, the calculator converts the signed result minutes to decimal hours using division by 60 and multiplies that exact value by the hourly rate. The visible decimal-hours value is rounded only for display, up to four decimal places.

Use the right calculator for the input you have

If you already know durations and want to add, subtract or convert them, stay on this page. If you have clock-in and clock-out times, use the Time Duration Calculator or Work Hours Calculator. If your source uses payroll decimals and you only need conversion, use the Decimal Hours Calculator.
