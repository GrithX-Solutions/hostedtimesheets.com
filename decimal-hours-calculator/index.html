Quick answer

Convert minutes to decimal hours by dividing minutes by 60 and adding the whole hours. For example, 8 hours 30 minutes equals 8.50 decimal hours.

Decimal hours = hours + (minutes ÷ 60)

How to convert minutes to decimal hours

Divide the minutes by 60, then add the result to the whole hours.

Decimal hours = hours + (minutes ÷ 60)

Common payroll conversions

15 minutes = 0.25 hours, 30 minutes = 0.50 hours, and 45 minutes = 0.75 hours.

Why decimal hours are used

Decimal time makes multiplication by an hourly rate straightforward. It is commonly used when preparing time records, invoices and payroll inputs.

More conversion examples

10 minutes is about 0.17 hours, 20 minutes is about 0.33, 40 minutes is about 0.67, and 50 minutes is about 0.83 when rounded to two decimal places.

Do not confuse clock notation with decimal time

A value such as 7:45 on a clock means 7 hours and 45 minutes, while 7.45 decimal hours means 7 hours plus 45 hundredths of an hour, which is 7 hours 27 minutes. Convert the minutes before multiplying by an hourly rate.

Rounding

This tool shows decimal results for convenient time and pay calculations. If your payroll, billing or timekeeping process specifies a particular rounding method, apply that method consistently to the source records.

FAQ

Is 8:30 equal to 8.30 decimal hours?

No. Thirty minutes is half an hour, so 8 hours 30 minutes equals 8.50 decimal hours.

What is 20 minutes in decimal hours?

20 ÷ 60 = 0.3333, normally shown as 0.33 when rounded to two decimals.

How do I convert decimal hours back to minutes?

Multiply the decimal fraction by 60. For example, 0.75 hour × 60 = 45 minutes.

Decimal time

Decimal hours are base-60 time expressed as a base-10 number

The conversion is useful for payroll, billing and spreadsheets, but the digits after the decimal point are not clock minutes.

Use minutes ÷ 60, not minutes ÷ 100

Convert the minute portion by dividing it by 60, then add the whole hours. For 8 hours 18 minutes, 18 ÷ 60 = 0.30, so the result is 8.30 decimal hours. For 8 hours 30 minutes, 30 ÷ 60 = 0.50, so the result is 8.50.

Why clock notation can be misleading

A display such as 8:45 means eight hours and forty-five minutes. Writing 8.45 as a decimal means eight hours plus 0.45 of an hour, which is 27 minutes. The correct decimal for 8:45 is 8.75 because 45 minutes is three quarters of an hour.

Payroll example with several daily durations

Suppose a week contains 8h 15m, 7h 30m, 8h 45m, 8h 00m and 7h 30m. Their decimal equivalents are 8.25, 7.50, 8.75, 8.00 and 7.50. Added together, the week is 40.00 hours. Converting each duration correctly prevents small minute errors from accumulating.

Keep more precision during intermediate calculations

Some minute values repeat in decimal form: 10 minutes is 0.1666… hours and 20 minutes is 0.3333…. When a payroll or billing system specifies a precision, keep enough digits during the calculation and round at the required stage. Rounding every individual value too early can change the final total.

Convert a decimal back to hours and minutes

Keep the whole-number hours, multiply the decimal fraction by 60, and round only as required. For 7.75 hours, the fraction 0.75 × 60 = 45 minutes, so the time duration is 7h 45m.

This calculator performs mathematical conversion only. A payroll system may use its own rounding or timekeeping rules, so compare the converted value with the system's documented format before importing or submitting it.
